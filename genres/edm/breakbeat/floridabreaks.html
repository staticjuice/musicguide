FLORIDA BREAKS

1993

Breakbeat kicked off as a style on its own with Florida Breaks. No rap lyrics, no breakdancing, almost no Hip Hop whatsoever – simply one long break digitally looped over and over. As one could expect, the music is sunny and uptempo, meant to mix together as one endless party breakbeat aimed at clubs. Florida Breaks is based on Electro, yet with stronger basslines. Just as the hard four-to-the-floor beat of Chicago House and Detroit Techno created a nightlife shockwave, so did the unheard of electronic breakbeats of Florida Breaks. Although the first records can be traced back to the late 80s, the Florida Breaks scene breaks loose around the mid-90s. Eventually, other artists picked up this music, got rid of the "Electro" effects, and focused on the breaks while adding lyrics. Thus Breakbeat gradually evolved from spontaneous mixing to DJ sets and finally professional productions with albums and performances. Funky Breaks uses more retro Funk and fewer Electro elements and forms the bridge between Florida Breaks and Big Beat.
